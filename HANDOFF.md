## What this is

A point-mass exterior ballistics calculator. Form inputs (load, sight setup, atmosphere, distance) produce two outputs: a range table showing velocity in fps, energy in ft-lbs, and height above/below the line of sight, and a chart of the projectile's arc from the muzzle out to the entered distance.

## What is already done and working

- **Solver** (`src/ballistics/solver.js`) — forward Euler integration at 0.25 ms steps, gravity plus drag. Line-of-sight coordinate frame: the bullet starts one sight height below the sight line, and the launch angle is found by secant iteration so the arc crosses zero at the zero range. Exposes `solveTrajectory`, plus `sampleAt`, `machCrossing`, and `sightLineCrossings`.
- **Drag tables** (`src/ballistics/dragTables.js`) — BRL-tabulated G1 (79 points) and G7 (84 points) Cd-vs-Mach curves. Generated data, not hand-written. Do not edit them by hand.
- **Atmosphere** (`src/ballistics/atmosphere.js`) — density and speed of sound from station pressure and temperature, plus an ICAO standard-atmosphere helper used only to prefill fields.
- **UI** (`src/App.jsx`, `src/components/`) — input panel, summary strip, Recharts trajectory chart with transonic/subsonic shading, and the range table. Purely presentational; no physics in components.
- **Tests** (`npm test`) — compares the solver against fixtures generated by `py-ballisticcalc`, an independent RK4 implementation, across three cartridges out to 1000 yards. Worst deviations currently: launch angle 0.0001 deg, velocity 0.9 fps, height 0.11 in, time 0.0004 s. The generator is `test/fixtures/generate.py`.

**Verify the baseline before changing anything:** run `npm install`, then `npm test`. It should print "All checks passed." If it doesn't, stop and tell me — something is wrong with the environment, not the code.
